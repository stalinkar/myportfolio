About Me

"Behind every seamless user experience is a tester who saw the bug before you did. Software quality is not just about testing—it’s about trust, automation, and enabling speed without compromise."

Hey there! I’m Stalin Kar, a Sr. SDET with nearly a decade of experience in ensuring financial applications are not just functional—but flawless. Whether it’s building robust UI frameworks or automating complex API workflows, I bring precision, performance, and passion to the testing table.
Currently at Broadridge Financial Solutions, I specialize in building scalable automation solutions using Selenium, Playwright, Java, Python, and Rest Assured. I drive in-sprint automation, maintain expansive regression suites, and build custom tools that simplify testing and data validation. My impact? A 60% reduction in manual effort and a 40% improvement in post-release quality.
I’ve worked across fintech and insurance domains, managing QA for mission-critical apps like from asset management to claims processing. I thrive in Agile teams, mentor junior testers, and bridge the gap between dev, QA, and business stakeholders.
Beyond the code, I’m an advocate for quality-first culture, agile practices, and mentoring teammates. Whether it's collaborating on test strategy or streamlining test data management, I bring both technical depth and team spirit to the table.
What sets me apart? I don’t just run tests—I engineer solutions. I’ve built custom VBA, Java, HTML and JS tools for test data management, improved code quality through Git reviews, and reduced release cycle time by streamlining QA processes.
🎯 Career-wise, I’m focused on growing into a QA leadership role where I can drive strategic automation and quality engineering initiatives across global teams.
💡 Fun fact: I’m not just about the code—I’m also a lifelong learner. I’m always exploring new tools and techniques to enhance test automation and improve software quality.
